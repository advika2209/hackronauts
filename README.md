#  MedThread
### AI-Powered Patient Story Summariser & Clinical Decision Support System

 "The LLM extracts. Clinical rules decide. Doctors verify."

---

## Problem

An elderly patient often consults multiple specialists, each maintaining separate medical records. Prescriptions, discharge summaries, and laboratory reports become scattered across hospitals and clinics, making it difficult for any single doctor to obtain a complete picture during a short consultation.

This fragmented view increases the risk of:

- Polypharmacy (multiple medications)
- Drug interactions
- Duplicate prescriptions
- Potentially inappropriate medications
- Missed laboratory follow-ups
- Prescribing cascades

Healthcare providers spend valuable consultation time manually reading documents instead of making clinical decisions.

---

## 💡 Solution

**MedThread** transforms scattered medical documents into a single, structured patient story.

Users upload prescriptions, discharge summaries, and laboratory reports. The system extracts relevant medical information, consolidates medications across all documents, attributes each medication to its prescribing doctor, and screens the unified record against established clinical criteria.

Unlike traditional AI summarisers, every extracted fact remains traceable to its original source document, allowing clinicians to verify information with a single click.

## Explainable AI

MedThread deliberately limits the role of AI.

The Large Language Model is responsible only for extracting structured medical information from uploaded documents.

All clinical warnings are generated by deterministic rule engines based on published medical guidelines.

Every extracted medication, laboratory value, allergy, or diagnosis is linked back to the exact location inside the original uploaded document through a provenance viewer.

This approach prioritizes transparency, clinician trust, and verifiability.

---

## Clinical Criteria Implemented

### AGS Beers Criteria
Identifies potentially inappropriate medications for adults aged 65 years and older.

### Anticholinergic Cognitive Burden (ACB) Scale
Calculates cumulative cognitive burden caused by medications with anticholinergic properties.

### Pre-operative Medication Hold Guidelines
Determines medications that should be temporarily discontinued before surgery based on published recommendations.

> Note: This prototype implements a curated subset of commonly encountered medications. A production system would integrate with a licensed clinical drug database.

## Patient View

Designed for patients and caregivers.

Instead of displaying clinical terminology, MedThread explains findings using clear, accessible language.

Includes:

- Your Medicines
- Things to Discuss With Your Doctor
- Questions You Can Ask
- Health Story Timeline
- Before Surgery Checklist

## Why MedThread?

A doctor rarely misses a diagnosis because one document is confusing.

More often, they miss what **no single document says on its own**.

MedThread connects fragmented medical records into one coherent story, helping clinicians identify patterns, risks, and absences that become visible only when every record is viewed together.
